SANGAM

Muzaffarabad ka pehla community flood aur landslide warning network

Muzaffarabad do dariyaon ke sangam par majood hai, jahan khatra badhne par shehar do hisson mein bat jata hai. Sangam ek community-run warning system hai — log khud report karte hain, aur alert mosque ke loudspeaker tak pohanch jata hai, koi smartphone zaroori nahi.

Why this matters

Muzaffarabad's crossings get cut off by both floods and landslides — flash floods and landslides across AJK and GB killed ten people in 2025 alone. The emergency flow exists for the same reason: when a crossing is cut, reaching CMH or AIMS becomes a matter of time.
